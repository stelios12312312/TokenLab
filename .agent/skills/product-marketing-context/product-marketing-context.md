# Product Marketing Context — Tesseract Academy

*Last updated: 2026-04-14*

## Product Overview
**One-liner:** Tesseract Academy helps executives and teams become AI-fluent through hands-on bootcamps, coaching, and strategic advisory.
**What it does:** Delivers AI education programs (bootcamps, clinics, executive coaching) that bridge the gap between AI hype and practical business application. Led by Dr. Stylianos Kampakis (PhD, 15+ years in data science and AI).
**Product category:** Executive AI Education & Training
**Product type:** Service + Digital (live bootcamps, online courses, 1:1 coaching)
**Business model:** Course fees (individual and B2B team licenses), coaching retainers, consulting engagements

## Target Audience
**Target companies:** Mid-to-large enterprises, scale-ups, and professional services firms looking to upskill leadership on AI strategy
**Decision-makers:** CEOs, CTOs, Chief Data Officers, HR/L&D Directors, Heads of Innovation
**Primary use case:** Equipping non-technical executives with the AI literacy to make informed strategic decisions
**Jobs to be done:**
- Understand what AI can and can't do for their business
- Build an AI strategy without depending entirely on technical teams
- Evaluate AI vendors and solutions with confidence
**Use cases:**
- Executive team AI fluency workshops
- Individual career acceleration through AI mastery
- Company-wide AI literacy programs

## Problems & Pain Points
**Core problem:** Executives know AI matters but lack the practical understanding to lead AI initiatives confidently.
**Why alternatives fall short:**
- Generic online courses are too shallow and lack strategic context
- Technical bootcamps are too deep and irrelevant for decision-makers
- Internal training takes months to develop and lacks external practitioner perspective
**What it costs them:** Missed opportunities, poor vendor decisions, competitive disadvantage, wasted AI investment
**Emotional tension:** Fear of being left behind, imposter syndrome around AI conversations, frustration with hype vs. reality

## Differentiation
**Key differentiators:**
- Led by Dr. Kampakis: real practitioner with PhD + 15 years, not a content creator
- Strategic focus: designed for decision-makers, not developers
- Practical outcomes: participants leave with actionable AI strategies, not just theory
- Small cohort sizes: personalized attention and real interaction
**Why customers choose us:** Credibility of the instructor, strategic (not technical) focus, proven outcomes from past cohorts

## Brand Voice
**Tone:** Authoritative but approachable, confident but not arrogant
**Style:** Direct, clear, practical. No jargon for jargon's sake.
**Personality:** Expert, pragmatic, forward-thinking, no-nonsense

## Words to Avoid (Slop Policy)
- "Synergy," "leverage," "best-in-class," "leading provider"
- "I hope this email finds you well"
- "Unlock," "empower," "revolutionize," "game-changer"
- Em-dashes
- Any reference to "learning to code" or "no-code" as a benefit (This is a 2017 framing; AI fluency is about workflow strategy, not avoiding programming).
- Any pattern that sounds like generic AI-generated marketing copy
