# Five Design Rules for Z1 Tokenomics

## Rule 1: Set the topup trigger aggressively
Sensitivity to WHEN you recapitalise is 3× higher than to inflow amount.

## Rule 2: Don't over-capitalise the AR at launch
A giant starting reserve creates a false sense of security. Negative elasticity (−0.77).

## Rule 3: Brand inflow is the oxygen
Below 1% of AR per epoch, the system collapses in every observed case.

## Rule 4: Constitutional 25% AR floor must be enforced mechanically
Don't trust governance to maintain it.

## Rule 5: Monitor passive viewer cohort share
If extractors grow, raise their settlement friction.

## One-Sentence Summary
> "Trigger early, right-size the reserve, secure external revenue, enforce the floor in code, and watch your cohort mix."
