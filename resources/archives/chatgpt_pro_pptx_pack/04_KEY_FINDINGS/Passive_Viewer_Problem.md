# The Passive Viewer Problem

## Cohort Economics

| Cohort | Settle Propensity | Spend Rate | Settle/Spend Ratio | Status |
|--------|------------------:|-----------:|-------------------:|:------:|
| Passive Viewers | 0.25 | 0.10 | **2.50×** | ⚠ Net Extractor |
| Active Viewers | 0.30 | 0.40 | **0.75×** | ⚠ Net Extractor |
| Power Users | 0.15 | 0.80 | **0.19×** | ✓ Net Contributor |

**Target:** settle/spend ≤ 0.5× per cohort.

## Why the System Survives
1. Power users massively subsidise passive viewers (0.19× vs 2.50×)
2. Brand inflow covers the remaining gap
3. 60/30/10 population split keeps passive viewers small in aggregate

## The Risk
Any growth strategy shifting the cohort mix toward extractors is structurally dangerous:
- Referral bonuses attracting speculators
- Airdrops to passive audiences
- Gamification that doesn't drive utility spend

## Recommendation
Tie settlement friction to cohort behaviour, not just global caps.
